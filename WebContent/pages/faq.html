What is the RTI Act?
The Right to Information Act (RTI) allows any Indian citizen to request information from a "public authority" which is required to reply within thirty days.
How to report a bribe request?
You can use our 'Report an Issue' section under the 'Corruption' category or contact the Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) directly.
